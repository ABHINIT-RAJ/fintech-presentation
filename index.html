Clean Money App
Clearer Decisions.

Clean Money App is a unified workspace for Indian small businesses to track billing, inventory, collections, and working capital with AI-powered CFO insights.

Launch Full Presentation Try Interactive Demo ↓
The Industry Problem

Why Small Businesses Struggle?

Traditional accounting tools only show past sales. They fail to give real-time insights into cash flow, blocked inventory, and pending collections.

📉

Disconnected Data

Sales, inventory, and expense records are scattered, making it impossible to see true financial health.

⏳

Delayed Collections

Lack of automated follow-ups leads to huge amounts of cash being stuck in unpaid invoices.

盲

Blind Decisions

Owners make crucial purchasing decisions without knowing their actual working capital shortages.

The Clean Money App Solution

Data Driven Decision Intelligence

✓

Unified Financial Ledger

Automatically connects sales, purchases, and expenses to calculate gross margins in real-time.

✦

AI CFO Command Center

Generates morning briefs, flags profit leakages, and advises on stock-tied cash dynamically.

System Architecture

  • Frontend: Next.js 16 (App Router)
  • Database: PostgreSQL (Neon)
  • ORM: Prisma Client
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS v4
  • Security: Blockchain Auditing Trail
